# DivalHR Product Definition

## Vision

DivalHR is the workforce operating platform for African organizations. It connects people administration, field operations, payroll preparation, organizational intelligence, and optional employee financial services.

## Launch market

The first country is the Democratic Republic of the Congo. The product must support French and English equally, CDF and USD, intermittent connectivity, multi-site organizations, and configurable country rules.

## Target customers

Organizations with approximately 50–2,000 workers in:

- Healthcare
- Education
- NGOs and development organizations
- Financial services
- Manufacturing

## Primary users

- HR administrators
- Operations and site managers
- Payroll and finance officers
- Executives
- Supervisors
- Employees
- Auditors
- Approved bank and MFI partners

## Product modules

### People

Employees, employment history, contracts, organizational assignments, leave, onboarding, documents, and lifecycle changes.

### Operations

Sites, teams, shifts, attendance, timesheets, tasks, expenses, allowances, approvals, and offline field workflows.

### Payroll

Approved pay inputs, pay periods, earnings and deductions, variance review, exports, and later country-validated calculations.

### Dival AI

Grounded workforce questions, daily summaries, reports, anomaly explanations, document extraction, and policy retrieval.

### Finance

Employee consent, employment verification, partner offers, application status, disbursement references, repayment status, and complaints. DivalHR does not make lending decisions.

## Product principles

1. French and English are first-class languages.
2. Build for African operating conditions from the beginning.
3. Configure country rules instead of creating country-specific application forks.
4. Require human approval for employment, payroll, disciplinary, and lending decisions.
5. Enforce authorization before data retrieval, including AI retrieval.
6. Build documented APIs and reliable business events from the beginning.
7. Support essential field workflows when connectivity is weak.
8. Start with a focused MVP and earn expansion through customer use.

## MVP

The MVP includes:

- Tenant and legal-entity configuration
- Sites, departments, teams, and roles
- Employee and employment records
- Contracts and employee documents
- Leave requests and approvals
- Shift scheduling
- Online and offline attendance
- Timesheets
- Tasks
- Expenses and allowances
- Payroll-input preparation and export
- Operational dashboards
- Notifications and one communications connector
- Grounded AI summaries with source links

Full multi-country payroll and Finance lending integrations are not MVP requirements.

## Success measures

- Time required to onboard an employee
- Attendance correction rate
- Payroll preparation time
- Payroll-input error rate
- Weekly active managers and supervisors
- Employee self-service adoption
- Offline synchronization success
- Uncovered shifts and overdue approvals
- AI grounded-answer and citation accuracy
- Pilot-to-paid conversion
