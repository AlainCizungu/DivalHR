# DivalHR Roadmap

## Phase 0 Discovery and Design Partners

Duration: 4–6 weeks

- Select two priority sectors and recruit one design partner in each.
- Map onboarding, attendance-to-payroll, leave, expenses, and one sector workflow.
- Validate French and English terminology with real users.
- Inventory DRC payroll, labor, privacy, and financial-services rules.
- Confirm DIP design tokens and component direction.
- Approve the MVP backlog, threat model, data model, and pilot outcomes.

Exit: two committed design partners and an approved MVP scope.

## Phase 1 Platform Foundation

Duration: 6–8 weeks

- Repository and delivery conventions
- Tenant and legal-entity foundation
- Authentication, MFA, roles, and audit
- Localization framework
- Design system
- API standards
- CI/CD and environments
- Observability and backups

Exit: a secure tenant shell deploys reproducibly.

## Phase 2 MVP

Duration: 12–16 weeks

- Employee and employment records
- Contracts and documents
- Organization, sites, departments, and teams
- Leave and approvals
- Scheduling and rotations
- Online and offline attendance
- Timesheets and costing
- Tasks
- Expenses and allowances
- Payroll-input preparation and export
- Dashboards and exception queues
- One communications connector
- Grounded AI daily summary

Exit: a design partner runs weekly operations in DivalHR with stable synchronization and traceable payroll exports.

## Phase 3 Payroll and Intelligence V1

Duration: 10–14 weeks

- Pay groups and periods
- Variance review and maker-checker approval
- Payslip or payroll export
- Approved DRC payroll calculations after expert validation
- Policy assistant
- Contract and credential alerts
- Monthly management reports
- ERP or accounting connector

Exit: two reconciled payroll cycles and measured user adoption.

## Phase 4 Industry Packs

Duration: 8–12 weeks

Deliver the two industry packs selected during discovery.

Exit: repeatable deployment and configuration for each selected sector.

## Phase 5 Finance Pilot

Duration: 12–20 weeks after legal and product gates

- Employee consent
- Partner onboarding
- Eligibility request
- Application and offer status
- Disbursement reference
- Repayment status
- Reconciliation
- Complaints and corrections
- Responsible-lending monitoring

Exit: legal approval, partner certification, employee-protection review, and a controlled pilot.

## Explicit sequencing rule

Do not build several country payroll engines, five industry packs, native mobile applications, bank integrations, and numerous independently deployed services simultaneously.
