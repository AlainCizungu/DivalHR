# Product reel: transcript and captions (LAUNCH-004, Issue #228)

The reel on the commercial page is silent, so there is no audio track and nothing to mute. It plays the real captures of the deployed application in sequence with a caption on each scene. It is not a video file: the page changes scenes itself (6 scenes × 5 seconds = 30 seconds), starts only when it scrolls into view, never starts for visitors who prefer reduced motion, and has a visible play and pause control. The captions below are the same text the page shows, and the page offers this transcript in a "Show the transcript" disclosure.

Source of the pictures: the captures listed in `docs/COMMERCIAL-SCREENSHOT-MANIFEST.md` (the scene 2 to 5 images). Scenes 1 and 6 are title cards with the "Controlled pilot" status.

## English

1. **0–5 s.** DivalHR: bilingual workforce and operations for African organizations.
2. **5–10 s.** An administrator sees quick actions and contracts needing attention at a glance.
3. **10–15 s.** The employee directory lists every employee with their employment status.
4. **15–20 s.** Payroll preparation produces payroll inputs only: no gross pay, tax or payment is calculated.
5. **20–25 s.** Employees reach their schedule and leave from their phone.
6. **25–30 s.** Controlled pilot. Request a demo.

## Français

1. **0–5 s.** DivalHR : gestion du personnel et des opérations, bilingue, pour les organisations africaines.
2. **5–10 s.** Un administrateur voit d’un coup d’œil les actions rapides et les contrats à traiter.
3. **10–15 s.** L’annuaire liste chaque employé avec son statut d’emploi.
4. **15–20 s.** La préparation de la paie ne produit que des données de préparation : ni salaire brut, ni impôt, ni paiement n’est calculé.
5. **20–25 s.** Les employés accèdent à leur horaire et à leurs congés depuis leur téléphone.
6. **25–30 s.** Pilote contrôlé. Demandez une démonstration.

## Notes

1. Scene 3 uses the employee directory capture, in which employee numbers and names are placeholders (see the manifest).
2. The payroll caption states the limit, not a capability: payroll inputs only, nothing calculated or paid.
3. Because the reel is made of the same lazily loaded images as the gallery, it adds no video or audio download and is never on the critical path.
