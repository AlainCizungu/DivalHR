# ADR 0003 Use a Partner Led Financial Services Model

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Employee salary advances may become a key differentiator, but direct lending would introduce substantial capital, licensing, underwriting, consumer-protection, and collections obligations.

## Decision

DivalHR provides consent, verified employment data exchange, workflow, status, and reconciliation. A licensed bank or microfinance institution performs underwriting, makes the decision, funds the product, provides disclosures, and manages regulated lending obligations.

## Consequences

Partner integration and contractual controls become critical. DivalHR reduces direct regulatory and capital exposure but still requires privacy, consent, fairness, complaint, and data-sharing controls.
